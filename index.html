LongiBot

LongiBot is a personal command-line tool for working with a Google account's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Contacts from a terminal.

It runs on its user's own computer and calls Google's APIs directly from there. It has no server, no hosted service and no sign-up. It is used by its developer and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Reads, writes and sends mail, attachments included.
  • Reads and manages calendar events.
  • Reads, uploads and organizes files in Google Drive.
  • Reads and updates contacts.

It reaches an account only after the account's owner signs in with Google and grants access, and it stops when they sign out of the tool or remove its access in their Google Account.
